System of units :  A complete set of units, both fundamental and 
derived for all kinds of physical quantities is called system of units. The 
common systems are given below  
(1) CGS system : This system is also called Gaussian system of units. 
In this length, mass and time have been chosen as the fundamental 
quantities and corresponding fundamental units are centimetre ( cm), gram 
(g) and second (s) respectively. 
(2) MKS system : This system is also called Giorgi system. In this 
system also length, mass and time have been taken as fundamental 
quantities, and the corresponding fundamental units are metre, kilogram 
and second. 
(3) FPS system : In this sys tem foot, pound and second are used 
respectively for measurements of length, mass and time. In this system force 
is a derived quantity with unit poundal. 
(4) S. I. system : It is known as International system of units, and is 
extended system of units appli ed to whole physics. There are seven 
fundamental quantities in this system. These quantities and their units are 
given in the following table
